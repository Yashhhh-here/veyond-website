We make businesses
look better.

An integrated team for businesses ready to build a stronger presence.

Veyond is an agency / studio built around a simple idea: a business should not have to coordinate five different freelancers to build one coherent digital presence.


We bring website design, graphic design, social media setup, content, video, advertising and Management Information Systems together as one connected solution. Our sector-specific plans are designed especially for schools and institutes, firms, cafés and restaurants, hotels and homestays, and stores.


The goal is practical: help a business establish and strengthen its digital and operational presence without making the process unnecessarily complicated or expensive.
