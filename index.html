06 / Pricing, simply

Pay for the
system you need.

Veyond pricing is split into two layers: an industry-specific integrated package and optional additional service packages. The integrated package covers the defined foundation; advanced functionality is scoped separately.

01

Integrated = foundation. MIS + professional website + defined graphic design + launch/handover.

02

Add-ons = expansion. Booking, ordering, e-commerce, advanced dashboards, branding and other advanced work are optional.

03

Social = optional. Setup and monthly management are not mandatory inside the integrated package.

04

Scope stays finite. Pages, modules, revision rounds, deliverables, timeline, ownership and exclusions should be specified.
